How it works (short version)

Four agents run on a shared Formulation Graph. Nothing is attached to a generic ingredient name. Everything is bound to a specific form (species + plant part + process + marker + assay).

  • Brief — turns your idea into a resolved, constraint-checked specification.
  • Grid — matches the form to lines that have run it (or close equivalents).
  • Proof — defines the analytical package that actually proves identity and strength.
  • Dossier — checks permissibility and claim support for the target market.

This is why problems that usually appear on the pilot line surface in the first 48 hours instead.

Why this is different from a normal CDMO conversation

One dietary status, everywhere

All-vegetarian lines. No animal-derived material, no cross-contact pathway. Cleaner claims and simpler change control.

We own the science, not the steel

Our bench + qualified partner lines. Specifications stay portable. You are not locked to one factory’s equipment list.
