Why the warehouse project never finishes.

Every mid-market company has built a warehouse, a lake, a lakehouse and a semantic layer. Each needed pipelines, schema work and a team to maintain them. The six-month project is always six more months from done.

  • No join keys

    CRMs, ERPs, spreadsheets and SFTP drops describe the same customers and orders under different column names. The join keys were supposed to live in a spec nobody wrote.

  • Five schemas, one entity

    The same customer exists in five systems with five slightly different shapes. Every downstream report picks one and prays, and reconciliation is wrong by morning.

  • Engineers hired elsewhere

    Senior data engineers are scarce and busy building at companies ten times your size. A mid-market ops team cannot carry a data platform group just to get the numbers right.

  • Enterprise tools, mid-market budget

    Platforms designed for a hundred data engineers arrive with the licensing, the services contract and the eighteen-month rollout.

  • Agents ready, data not

    An ops lead can prototype a useful agent over a weekend. The agent asks a simple question, and six months later the data layer still cannot answer it.

Six months of data engineering, done in days.

How a thesis gets written.

The same method runs in every market, which is why the pages read alike and the conclusions do not.

  • Map the profit pool

    Total industry profit by activity rather than by company, so you can see where margin concentrates across the chain instead of who currently holds it.

  • Sort by AI exposure

    Displaced, accelerated or augmented. Pattern‑matching functions go first; anything needing earned trust goes last.

  • Sequence the shift

    Which activity to rebuild first, why the order is causal, and where the margin compounds rather than leaks.

  • Write the position

    Who gains, who loses, on what timeline. A position, not a survey of use cases.

The mark

MOAT carries the weight.

Spacetime curves bend through the letterforms, with nodes where they cross. MOAT is solid; IVE is drawn in outline. The mark holds a moat and a gravitational field in the same shape.

Clear space

Keep free space equal to the height of the O on every side. Never crowd the mark.

Minimum size

22px tall on screen, 12mm in print. Below that the nodes and curves stop reading.

Do not

Do not recolour, outline, rotate, add effects, or reconstruct the letterforms.

One accent, and it only marks action.

Terracotta marks things a person can click, and the unit on a number. It is never decoration, and never the wordmark.

  • What we collect

    Your name, work email, company and message from the contact form. Aggregate analytics on which pages are read. Technical logs for security. Nothing else.

  • Why

    To reply, to see which material is useful, and to keep the site secure. Where we rely on consent you can withdraw it at any time.

  • Who sees it

    Our hosting, email and analytics providers, under written terms, only to do their job. We do not sell or rent personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

  • How long

    Enquiries for up to 24 months from last contact. Technical logs for up to 90 days. Engagement data follows the signed agreement.

  • Where

    We operate in India and the United States, so data may be processed in both, under standard contractual clauses or an equivalent safeguard.

  • Your rights

    Ask for a copy, a correction or deletion. Object to processing or withdraw consent. Write to hub@moative.com and we reply within 30 days.

  • Cookies

    Only what the site needs to work, plus non-identifying analytics you can block without losing anything. Typefaces load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address.

  • Sub‑processors

    We use a small set of providers for hosting, email, analytics and scheduling. The current list, and notice of any change, is available on request from hub@moative.com.

  • Data processing agreement

    Where we process personal data on your behalf during an engagement, a DPA with standard contractual clauses is available on request and forms part of the signed agreement.

  • Breach notification

    If a breach affects your personal data we will notify you and the relevant authority without undue delay, and in any case within the period the applicable law requires.

  • Not for children

    This site is aimed at businesses. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. Write to us if you believe we have and we will delete it.

    Complaints under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 go to hub@moative.com.
