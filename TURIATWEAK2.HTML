The practical difference

Problems caught before the bench

These are the failures that normally surface only at pilot. We surface them in the first pass.

Payload arithmetic

A 3 g gummy carries about 3 g. 400 mg elemental magnesium as bisglycinate is already ~2.8 g of salt. The system flags it and proposes a split dose or format change.

Process survivability

Most lactobacilli die at depositing temperature. Spore-formers survive. The system eliminates the wrong organism instead of discovering it on the line.

Dietary status integrity

All-vegetarian on every line. No animal-derived material in carriers, glazes or processing aids. Cross-contact pathways are designed out.

Form-level evidence

Nothing attaches to a generic ingredient name. Evidence is bound to the exact form (species + part + process + marker). Silent promotion is blocked.

Platform

Four agents. One graph.

Turia Core runs four specialised agents against a shared Formulation Graph. Every fact is typed, sourced, and graded. Nothing is attached to a generic ingredient name.

Brief
Turns the human request into a resolved specification. Performs name resolution, dose math, dietary propagation and first-pass constraint checks.
Grid
Matches the resolved form to manufacturing lines that have actually run similar matrices — using licences, filings and production history, not marketing claims.
Proof
Defines the analytical package that proves identity and strength for the specific form in use. Methods are tied to the form, not the category.
Dossier
Checks US permissibility and structure/function support component-by-component. Where the record is silent, it marks the fact UNCLEAR rather than inventing an answer.

Form-level discipline

The graph does not treat “ashwagandha” as a single object. It distinguishes root vs leaf, extraction process, marker and assay. Evidence that belongs to one form is never silently applied to another. This is the core of the system’s reliability.

What the graph carries

  • Canonical forms (species + part + process + marker + assay)
  • Constraint rules (payload, temperature, solubility, dietary status)
  • Evidence graded by strength and freshness
  • Line capability and history
  • Market permissibility and claim support
