SmartSalary — Employer-Agnostic Income Detection

Designed the core income-detection engine that works even when salary credits are fragmented, inconsistently labelled, or arrive from multiple sources — a common reality for Indian salaried customers.

Problem

Traditional salary detection broke on non-standard employer names, multiple accounts, or irregular credit patterns. This directly limited loan eligibility and disbursal rates for partner lenders.

Approach

Built a hybrid system: recurrence + exclusion rules for high-precision cases, augmented with ML for the long tail of fragmented data. Tight feedback loop with credit teams on false positives/negatives.

Impact

12% increase in client loan disbursals. Parallel Business Income model for self-employed clients drove an additional 6% uplift in SME & Personal Loan disbursals.

Product Thinking · Feature Spec Side project · Structured product exercise

Bumble — Smart Openers to Increase Conversation Start Rate

A deliberate product-thinking exercise: identify a high-leverage problem in the post-match funnel and design a measurable feature with clear success metrics, trade-offs, and guardrails.

Problem Framing

Industry data and user research consistently show that a large percentage of matches never turn into conversations. The first message is a high-friction moment: users overthink, fear looking generic, or simply don’t know what to say. This is especially acute among busy working professionals who have limited mental bandwidth for dating apps.

Insight

Profile prompts, bio, and photos already contain rich, under-utilised signals. Users respond better to low-effort, high-relevance openers that feel personal without being invasive or creepy.

Proposed Solution

Surface 3 AI-generated “Suggested Openers” after a match. Generated from shared interests, prompt answers, and photo context (with vision safety). User can edit, use, or ignore. Continuous feedback improves relevance.

Success Metrics & Guardrails

  • Primary: Conversation start rate (match → first message sent)
  • Secondary: Reply rate to first message, 7-day matched-pair retention, qualitative “felt natural” score
  • Guardrails: No generic “hey”, toxicity & harassment filter, cultural tone calibration for Indian market, opt-out always available, clear transparency that suggestions are AI-assisted

Trade-offs Explicitly Considered

Authenticity vs. conversion · Compute cost at scale · Potential bias in generation · Risk of making conversations feel less human · Data-use and privacy boundaries · Impact on women-first dynamic that is core to Bumble’s brand.

Product Teardown Analytical exercise · Growth & category opportunity

Swiggy Instamart — Daily Wellness & Supplements Opportunity

A structured teardown examining how a high-intent, high-repeat category (supplements, nutraceuticals, daily wellness) sits inside Instamart today and where product could unlock more value.

Observation

Quick commerce has already trained users to expect 10–20 minute delivery for groceries and essentials. Wellness products (protein, electrolytes, vitamins, Ayurvedic formulations, gut health) have high AOV, strong repeat potential, and currently fragmented discovery across 1mg, Amazon, local chemists, and brand D2C sites.

Current Gaps

  • Taxonomy & search do not prioritise “daily stack” mental models
  • Weak personalisation / goal-based entry points (Energy, Sleep, Gut, Focus)
  • Limited trust & education layer compared to pure-play wellness apps
  • Subscription / auto-replenish under-developed for this category
  • Bundle recommendations (e.g., electrolytes + protein) under-indexed

What I would prioritise

  • Dedicated Wellness aisle with curated “daily stacks”
  • Goal-based onboarding & personalisation surface
  • Flexible subscription + smart reminders with easy skip
  • Lightweight content + social proof to reduce purchase anxiety
  • Cross-sell into existing high-frequency grocery baskets

Why this matters as a PM exercise: It forces prioritisation under real constraints (existing IA, logistics SLAs, category margins, regulatory sensitivity around claims) while still looking for asymmetric upside in a high-LTV category.
