Understanding RAG

Retrieval-Augmented Generation is a technique that combines the power of retrieval systems with generative LLMs.

WHY RAG?
  • Reduces hallucinations
  • Provides up-to-date information
  • Grounds answers in real documents
  • Cost-effective compared to fine-tuning
